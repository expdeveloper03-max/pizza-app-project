Common questions

I can't find my order number

It is on the confirmation message from when you placed the order. If you have lost it, contact the restaurant and give the phone number you used.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 25 to 45 minutes, depending on how busy we are and how far you are from the kitchen.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change or cancel while the status is still "Order received" or "Preparing". After the pizza is in the oven, it can no longer be changed.

My order is late. What should I do?

Check the status above first. If it has been more than 15 minutes past the estimate, contact the restaurant and we will sort it out.
